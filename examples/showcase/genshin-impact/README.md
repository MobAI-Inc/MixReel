# Genshin Impact · run and production notes

Two unofficial concept ads for Genshin Impact (miHoYo), made for Douyin in Chinese at 9:16. A coding agent running MixReel made both, starting from an empty project folder.

The agent first wrote a brand file from official public sources only, the game's news site and its official Douyin account. Every claim in the films traces back to an official source. Both films went through a first test round in which the faces came out glossy and airbrushed. The people were recast and every live-action shot was generated again. These notes describe that second round, which is what shipped.

## Performance ad · "Finally in on it" (插上话了)

<video src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/305adf51-c6b3-4712-ae3f-545e185dd965" controls muted></video>

**Brief.** A Douyin in-feed ad in Chinese, aimed at people whose friends and coworkers play and who have not started yet. Asked for 15 to 30 seconds at 1080×1920. The cut runs 37.5 seconds.

**Selling point.** From version 7.2, a newly registered player can invite one standard five-star character of their choice. The offer comes from the official notice at https://ys.mihoyo.com/main/news/detail/167167, which limits it to「在7.2版本更新后完成注册且解锁祈愿功能的旅行者」(travelers who register after the 7.2 update and unlock Wishes). The same notice says「旅行者仅能邀请一次角色」(one invitation per traveler). Those conditions are too long for the picture, so they go in the ad's caption text. The film says「常驻」(standard) every time, so nobody reads it as a limited character. It never calls the offer free or guaranteed.

**Idea.** At lunch a coworker pitches the game to the quiet one yet again, then she and another longtime player lean in to replay a moment from the 7.2 trailer. He finishes their reaction for them, and it turns out he started a while ago and went straight to the newest region. The feeling is "it is not too late to get in." It borrows the sitcom beat where the person everyone wrote off says one insider line and the table goes quiet.

## Brand film · "This table of people" (这一桌人)

**Brief.** A Douyin brand film in Chinese for players of many years and the people around them. Asked for 30 to 45 seconds at 1080×1920. The cut runs 43.7 seconds.

**Selling point.** Version 7.2 is live, and its main story seats the leaders of all seven nations at one table. The line comes from the official image title at https://ys.mihoyo.com/main/news/detail/167238, which reads「「圣土化」蔓延，七国代表齐聚至冬」(as the blight spreads, envoys of the seven nations gather in Snezhnaya). The film keeps the official wording on the end card.

**Idea.** On the night of the update, a man who has played for six years tears up over his phone, and his girlfriend asks if he is not bored of it yet. He turns the screen to her and says he met everyone at that table one by one. It borrows the photo-album scene, where someone points at a face and names the year of their own life that goes with it.

## How both were made

Before any image was generated, the agent blocked each live-action scene in Blender with colour-coded mannequins, one scene file covering every shot, and checked the eyelines and screen direction from the renders. For each person, Midjourney made a realistic face. GPT Image then completed that face into a full 9:16 costume frame and left the face as it was. Every shot's first frame was built from those cast frames. MiniMax H3 Max Turbo animated each first frame at 1080p and spoke the dialogue itself, from prompts that gave each speaker an age and a way of talking. The real logo and official footage were composited in the edit, along with the subtitles and one stock music track from the Mixkit free library, Walking in the Park for the ad and Silent Descent for the brand film.

The ad was generated as thirteen single-shot clips. The brand film was covered like a live shoot, seven takes that each cover several cuts. His two voice-over lines were spoken on camera in a close-up and only the audio was kept.

Review ran on the finished cut. A local QA script checked the cut points and loudness and flagged any black frame or silent gap. The agent also looked through frame sheets of every shot and every seam. Gemini then transcribed each clip and the full mix by ear and judged whether the voices sounded human. Its transcript was compared with the script. That pass caught one real problem. In the ad, 你玩了？ ("you've played?") sounds the same as 你完了？ ("you're done for"), and Gemini heard the second both times, so the line became 你也在玩？ and the shot was generated again. In the same ad, the green-screen key on the phone also replaced a green potted plant in the background until it was limited to the phone's screen.

## Known flaws left in

**Finally in on it.**

- At the end of the first shot, the quiet one leans forward and partly covers the coworker's face. Her line has already finished by then.
- In the shots toward him, the water dispenser and microwave from her side of the room show up behind him as well. The background is soft, so it is easy to miss.
- The phone in the close-up near the end has a small mark on its back that the model drew.
- It runs 37.5 seconds against a brief of 30 at most. The generated dialogue came out slower than the first round's, even with the clips sped up 1.1 times.
- The trailer's line ends on a very soft 了. Gemini's full-mix transcript left it out, and the subtitle follows the official subtitle.
- The end card background is a frame from the landscape trailer cropped to vertical and enlarged, so it is soft. It sits darkened and blurred behind the text.

**This table of people.**

- Her first line plays over his close-up, before the film cuts to her.
- Venti and Aether come from the landscape trailer, cropped and enlarged about twice, and look soft next to the vertical shots.
