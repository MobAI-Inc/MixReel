# The idea

Before writing, read `brand/films.md`, and the approved scripts and rejected approaches in `brand/audience-taste.md`. Ads in `brand/competitors.md` and `brand/taste-library/` show which forms this viewer already knows. Any of these may be empty.

Pick one idea that makes the message visible. Write it as an event a camera can shoot, such as a hand, a product, a screen or a result. The idea is not a slogan glued on at the end.

Before any shot, write the theme in a sentence or two a stranger with no context gets at once. Say what the viewer should come away feeling, the form of the film, and the chain of events that gets her there. The product is how the feeling happens inside that chain, never the subject of the sentence.

Find the chain in a moment that already won a large audience instead of inventing it, such as a famous film scene, a widely shared clip or a long-running ad. Look at why so many people love it and borrow its mechanism, never its plot or names, played through this product and these people. The payoff lands in one continuous scene, minutes after its cause. Anything earlier is fine when one line, one caption or one shot carries it. A moment whose payoff needs the viewer to feel time pass, such as a skill trained over weeks, does not fit a short ad. When the product itself works across time, such as a reminder that arrives later, that gap is its premise and one caption carries it.

For the idea, record:

- the theme, and the moment you borrowed it from
- the opening image
- the turn, in one action
- the last image, and the copy on it
- why this viewer would recognize the form
- what you are not showing

Fill the idea section of [the script template](../assets/SCRIPT.template.md).

## When to pause

A user who asks for a script gets the whole script in one delivery, with the idea and the shot list together and marked as a draft. Do not stop between them. Asking for a script hands you the choice of idea for this draft. The user approves or rejects it when they read the result, and changing the idea means rewriting the shot list.

Stop at the idea only when the user asked to see ideas or alternatives first. Give more than one event, not more than one adjective list, wait for their pick, then write the shot list for that one.

Either way the approval that must come before any illustration is approval of the finished script.

Rejected directions stay rejected. Do not revive one by changing the caption. An approved or well-performing idea may be carried onto another product or story, as [project](../../mixreel/references/project.md#record-every-film) describes.
