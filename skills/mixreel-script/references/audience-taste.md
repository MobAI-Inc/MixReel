# Audience

Before you invent a structure, write down what this viewer already wants and which ad shapes they already understand. Use the brief, the reference analysis, and `brand/audience-taste.md` and `brand/competitors.md` when they have anything in them. Do not invent a demographic study.

Name:

- the situation the viewer is in when the ad starts
- the desire the product can honestly meet
- the familiar form (demo, unboxing, before/after, founder line, screen recording) you are using and why
- the point where a stranger would leave if the product has not appeared

If you cannot answer from these, ask one question. Do not fill the gap with a generic lifestyle scene. Write what you settled into `brand/audience-taste.md`, so the next film starts from it.
