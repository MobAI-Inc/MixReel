# Audience

Before you invent a structure, write down what this viewer already wants and which ad shapes they already understand. Use the brief and the reference analysis. Do not invent a demographic study.

Name:

- the situation the viewer is in when the ad starts
- the desire the product can honestly meet
- the familiar form (demo, unboxing, before/after, founder line, screen recording) you are using and why
- the point where a stranger would leave if the product has not appeared

If you cannot answer from the brief, ask one question. Do not fill the gap with a generic lifestyle scene.
