# When to block in 3D

Build a previs before you pay for generation for every scene told in more than one shot in one place, however simple. Shots generated one at a time drift in position, facing and screen direction, and the previs is where the whole scene is laid out first. Render every shot of the scene in one pass.

Typical cases:

- a product orbit or a turntable move
- hands entering frame to open, pour, or place
- several angles of the same pack or device that must match
- a screen recording whose frame must sit in a set

Skip it for a scene of a single shot, such as one locked-off pack shot, a caption card, or a recording you already have.

The previs is a blocking check. It is not the finished picture. Flat-shaded raster output is enough to see occlusion and screen direction. Blender is optional and sharper. See [how to render](previs-render.md).
