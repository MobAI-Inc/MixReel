# Borrow structure, not content

Script step 4, and only when there is a reference. The step list is [the script skill](../SKILL.md).

A reference is a file the user supplied for this film, or an ad they picked from `brand/taste-library/`. With neither, skip this file. Do not search for someone else's ad to imitate.

A reference can lend a hook shape, a pacing pattern, a camera move, or a music relationship. It cannot lend its footage, its track, its performer, its package, or its claims. Treat it as evidence of a particular viewing experience. Read the brief first, then relate the observed choices to that intent. One or more dimensions may be chosen. Copying the whole video is never required, and its content or script is not excluded automatically.

## Record the selection

For each relevant dimension record the source time and evidence, the useful mechanism, keep, adapt, exclude, or pending, the target treatment, the reason, and who decided. In an authorized autonomous proposal you may choose dimensions and explain your choices. Do not label that choice user-confirmed. Keep decisions already recorded. Unselected dimensions do not quietly control the new film.

| Dimension | What to examine |
| --- | --- |
| Content and composition | Observable event, subject positions and scale, spatial contrast, visual reveal, viewpoint |
| Script and information | What viewers know or expect at each point, exact copy and spoken lines, order of reveals, cause and reaction, what the ending resolves or holds back |
| Hook | The first thing noticed, the immediate reason to care, the question or expectation it opens, and the later payoff |
| Music and sound | Phrase, energy, vocals, lyric meaning, silence, recognizable sound or spoken delivery |
| Editing and motion | Cut spacing, pauses, acceleration, action accents, camera and performance behavior, and how they relate to the music |
| Style and presentation | Rendering, palette, lighting, typography, UI treatment, meme and genre conventions, and audience familiarity |

Use the dimensions relevant to this request. The table is not a quota. The reference's exact treatment stays below the user's recorded brand, identity, wardrobe, product, and rights constraints. A conflict produces a clear trade-off, not an unnoticed change. A borrowed composition does not bring its style with it.

## Explain attention through events

Describe the first meaningful event and what a viewer expects next. Name the shot, word, gesture, or sound that changes that expectation. Locate the payoff and whether it arrives before attention is likely to drift. Mark that judgment as a hypothesis, not a measured retention result.

Write the actual copy or action when you have it. Labels such as fast-paced, viral, emotional, or cinematic do not explain the mechanism. Borrow slang, memes, songs, and references that the [audience](audience-taste.md) record shows this audience already uses. Slang added without that evidence reads as imitation. Check that a joke, a cameo, or a nostalgic reference relies on knowledge the target viewer has, and adapt or replace it explicitly when it does not.

## Creative logic and musical timing

A reference's pauses, cuts, and reveals are tied to its sound. Its underlying idea may survive a new track. Keep the chosen idea while rebuilding the timeline against the target phrases, accents, spoken readability, and action duration. Do not scale all source timestamps mechanically or require a cut at every beat.

When the lyric, a recognizable sound, or silence is itself the joke or the reveal, record that dependency. A replacement track may need a rewritten hook or payoff. An excerpt the user chose stays fixed unless a conflict is raised. With no target audio, a full script still proposes a concrete sound treatment using the [early feasibility check](storyboard.md#early-feasibility-check). Exact alignment can stay provisional. An outline can explicitly defer this work.

Keep source observation, planned transfer, and final asset conditioning separate. For example, a creator's recorded reaction can inspire the new reveal order without becoming a submitted identity or style reference. Record the actual roles again at the image and video stages.

## Evidence limits

Captions in a document, thumbnails, and frame sheets are partial evidence. Do not infer a whole shot's motion or sound from them. If a dimension you need cannot be observed, say so before claiming the script reproduces it. User preference and supplied campaign results can guide choices. View counts alone do not prove conversion, paid reach, or which creative element caused performance.
