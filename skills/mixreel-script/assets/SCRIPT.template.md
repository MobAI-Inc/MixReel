# Script

## Intent and evidence

Artifact version, audience, purpose, the one message, the brand files used with their dates, product sources, confirmed constraints, and the requested stop point. Keep product claims and their conditions traceable to `brand/brand-context.md`. Mark fictional additions and evidence you could not get.

## Idea

One event. The theme, as [the idea](../references/marketing-idea.md#one-event-and-its-theme) describes it, the moment it borrows from, and any earlier film whose idea it reuses. The longing it serves, the familiar things it uses with their evidence, how the product appears, and the rights position of anything that belongs to others, such as the music. Opening image, turn, last image, copy on the last image. What this film refuses to show. Then describe the film as it plays. Alternatives only when requested.

## Message

The one message, the claim source, and the conditions that must stay visible.

## Why it should work

Against [the idea](../references/marketing-idea.md), what the viewer feels at each stretch, what holds them to the next, and how the product enters. Mark what comes from the user, audience evidence, a reference, and your own judgment.

One row is one beat, as [storyboard](../references/storyboard.md#beats-and-reactions) describes. A beat on the other person's side, or a separate action, takes another row. Write `none` where a shot has no copy or no spoken line.

Keep source timestamps apart from target times. Mark the target music excerpt, its phrases and accents, and your timing confidence, or give the proposed original or sound-only treatment and mark exact alignment provisional. Document intentional montage gaps.

## Assets

Real logo, package, product photo, and screen recording, each with the shot it supports. Missing assets stay listed as missing.

## Reference sheet

`reference-sheet.jpg` when real images exist, such as frames from the brand's taste library, product photos, and screenshots. Name the shot each image supports and its source.

## Previs

Renderer and paths, or "not used" and why.

## Early production feasibility

Edit length, the shots made as video, image, and post, known duration, input, and audio limits with their evidence, provisional groups when needed, boundary states, and sound continuity. Keep unknown request settings and prices unknown. Detailed verified execution belongs to video planning.

## Self-review and open items

Concrete revisions made after reading the sequence as a viewer who knows nothing. Unknown provider limits, unapproved claims, accepted and proposed decisions, supplied assets, missing exact UI or copy, and the next decision you need from the user. Do not invent user approval, a production plan, or a paid job.
