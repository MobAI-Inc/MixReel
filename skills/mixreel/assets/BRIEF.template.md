# Brief

## Request

Deliverable, audience, the feeling it should leave, length, aspect ratio, channel, and the step the user has authorized. State the current stop point.

## Sources

Reference videos, the target music or excerpt, product photos, screen recordings, logo or package files, and person consistency references. For each file record what it is allowed to contribute (hook, pacing, camera, product truth, voice, face, clothing, angles) and what it must not contribute. Record where it came from, an unchanging local path or hash, and any evidence that is missing. Keep keys and signed URLs out.

## Message

Link `brand/brand-context.md` and the other brand files this film relies on, with their dates. Record what is specific to this film: the one message it carries, the setting, the longing and familiar point the idea starts from, the campaign objective, the claim limits, the desired action, any required on-screen copy, and any test hypothesis. Product facts stay in the brand file.

## Borrowing

For each borrowed dimension: source time, keep / adapt / exclude / pending, the target treatment, why, and who decided. Record when music, lyrics, or sound carry the idea, and rebuild the edit timing when the track changes. Borrow structure. Do not copy someone else's footage, music, packaging, or words. Unselected dimensions do not quietly control appearance or wording.

## Decisions

Style source, provider and model id, previs yes or no, which shots are generated versus a real recording, the user-confirmed role of each input image type (composition, style, identity, wardrobe, product), and where each person's chosen wardrobe reference comes from. Generation groups and why they split, new keyframes versus reused assets, the roles of video and audio references, and duration and cost per request as quoted, estimated, or unknown. Note uncertainty where it affects the result.

## Reviews and budget

For each review: the artifact version or hash, who reviewed it, the decision (pass, fail, not checked), the evidence, and the scope it covers. Record currency, ceiling, per-job cap, retry limit, and any authorized experiments. Separate a delegated choice from a user approval.

## Current work

Link stage artifacts, the plan, the journal, provider profiles, and review findings when they exist. Script-only work does not fake production fields. Record accepted and rejected asset versions, open defects, and the next useful action. A new instruction updates this section before dependent work resumes.
