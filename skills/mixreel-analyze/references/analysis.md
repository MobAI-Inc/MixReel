# What to record

Write times as `MM:SS.sss`. Separate observation from inference.

Cover, when the media supports it:

- the hook in the first seconds, and what changes at each cut
- shot scale, camera move, and how the product or the UI enters
- on-screen copy, captions, and any spoken line
- music accents, silence, and where a cut sits relative to a hit
- what a new spot could borrow (structure, pacing, reveal) and what it must not borrow (identity, footage, claims, brand marks)

End with open questions. Do not score the reference and do not promise that a similar structure will perform.
