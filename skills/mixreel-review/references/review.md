# Review criteria

You are checking an artifact against its approved target. The stage card is [the review skill](../SKILL.md). How wide a repair is lives in [diagnosis](diagnosis.md). Brand marks live in [brand fidelity](../../mixreel/references/brand-fidelity.md).

Read the actual deliverable, its version, the current user requirements, and the selected reference roles. State what you could watch, hear, or read. A missing source limits comparison. It does not invalidate every finding you can observe.

| Artifact | Check against the approved target |
| --- | --- |
| Script | A concrete hook and payoff, information that changes, comprehension for a viewer who knows nothing, one message, claims and their conditions, the borrowed dimensions, readable copy or speech, the brand plan, target audio timing |
| Keyframe | Framing, subject scale and position, action, product or person match, the user-selected style and wardrobe, light and time, physical plausibility, reference-role leakage, unintended edits, real logo and package. For a scene of several shots, the frames played in order show the scene's event |
| Prompt | Approved event and end state, ordered references, documented capabilities, shot or group boundary, action speed, gaze and geography, exact copy assigned to the edit layer |
| Generated video | Actual camera and subject motion, contact and continuity through cuts, identity and style, pacing, gaze, required events, defects through the whole file |
| Master | In-points, action joins, music accents, dialogue and effects, captions, UI and brand layers, a readable ending, duration, audio coverage, export size |

Use the script skill's [ad checks](../../mixreel-script/references/ad-script.md) when reviewing a script. A working renderer or a completed generation status cannot answer these creative questions.

For a finished script, require shot ids, scale and viewpoint, action, blocking, camera, end states and joins, copy, and a sound plan, plus early handling of known generation limits. Require previs blocking when a previs was part of the plan. Do not accept a timed paragraph as the script. Check what the viewer wants and what the person on screen gets to do against the brief, and the hook-to-payoff mechanism against the reference evidence. A premise the user rejected stays a failed creative check.

Gaze matches the shot. A presenter who is supposed to look at the product should look at the product. A line to camera is a fail only when the shot said otherwise. Check that camera choices support the planned reveal or action instead of repeating a convenient default.

On-screen copy is read without production notes. Flag an internal review label that became a caption. Keep a disclosure the claim needs.

## Findings and closure

For each issue record the version and location (the time, for footage), the target, the observed difference, the impact, your confidence, the earliest likely cause, the narrow repair destination, and how to recheck. Separate evidence of a defect from a guess about its cause. Put failures that change the product's meaning, or make the result unusable, ahead of cosmetic preferences.

Use pass, fail, and not checked for each dimension that applies. No total score. An agent review is not user acceptance. Record accepted preferences without turning them into universal rules.

Repair authorization follows the original task and budget. A review-only task produces findings. A repair task invokes the responsible stage and keeps unaffected assets. Do not change provider, add a paid attempt, or rewrite the script just to close the review. A fail, or a cut that visibly leaves the script or the continuity of a person or product, does not finish a delivery. Follow the delivery rule in the review skill.

On each keyframe, check logos, packaging text, product copy, and app UI against the client asset, per brand fidelity.

After a repair, inspect the changed work for regressions, including regions inside a changed image that should have stayed still. Carry forward an unchanged image's pass only if it already passed and its requirements and references are unchanged. A pixel diff cannot approve a failed or unreviewed image. A repair with no effect stays failed. Record evidence and outcome per finding. Follow [diagnosis](diagnosis.md): rework changes only what needs changing. If the cause is still uncertain, or the new result does not improve, revise the diagnosis before another paid attempt.
