# Review criteria

Check the artifact against the approved brief. State what you could watch, hear, or read.

| Artifact | Check |
| --- | --- |
| Script | Hook, one message, claims and their conditions, borrowed dimensions, readable copy, brand plan |
| Keyframe | Framing, product or person match, action, light, unintended edits, real logo and package |
| Prompt | Approved event and end state, ordered references, documented capabilities, copy assigned to the edit rather than the model |
| Generated video | Camera and subject motion, continuity, identity, required events, defects through the whole file |
| Master | In-points, joins, music accents, captions, UI and brand layers, duration, audio, export size |

For a finished script, require shot ids, scale, action, copy, and sound. Require blocking when a previs was part of the plan. Do not accept a timed paragraph as the script.

Gaze matches the shot. A presenter who is supposed to look at the product should look at the product. A line to camera is a fail only when the shot said otherwise.

Use pass, fail, and not checked. No total score.

On-screen copy is read without production notes. Flag a note that became a caption. Keep a disclosure the claim needs.

Findings name the version, the time, the target, the difference, the impact, your confidence, the earliest cause, the repair stage, and how to recheck. Separate evidence from a guess about the cause.
