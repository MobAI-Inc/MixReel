# Video inspection

Judge the master, not a pile of selects.

1. Run `qa_check.py` so flash, flicker, cut offset, missed cuts, off-beat cuts, silence, loudness, and size are on paper.
2. Build a frame pack for the suspects and for brand frames.
3. Watch the master with sound when you can.
4. Write the note.

A detector time is a place to look. Confirm, reject, or mark uncertain. Pixel defects (a one-frame white flash, a stray frame of the previous shot) are settled on the frames. A line landing in the wrong shot, a look to the wrong place, or a product that never appears are settled on playback. If the frames and the playback disagree, write uncertain.

Off-beat means the cut is farther from the measured beat than the tolerance. It is not a taste comment about the track.

Brand frames are full resolution so a logo at the edge can be compared with the asset. A soft or substituted mark fails.

Optional models are hints under [review tools](review-tools.md). They do not replace this pass.
