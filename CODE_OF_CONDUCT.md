# Code of Conduct

MixReel is a working project. People who file issues, review changes, and
send pull requests are expected to treat one another with ordinary professional
respect.

## Expected

- Comment on the work, not the person.
- Assume a good-faith reading of a question before correcting it.
- Give enough detail that the next person can reproduce a bug.
- Accept a no on a change that would leak private material or break the license.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, slurs, or sexual comments.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Repeatedly pushing a change that maintainers have declined with a reason.

## Reports

Report conduct problems to vito_zhong@mobai-game.com. Include links, not a
second copy of private material. Maintainers may edit or close a thread, and
may block an account that keeps breaking these rules.
